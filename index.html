The problem

In Sri Lanka, people with skills and spare time struggle to find trusted paid work, while people with everyday tasks struggle to find reliable, verified help, because there is no single trusted place that connects them.

If you have skills

Students, part-time earners and skilled workers rely on word of mouth. They cannot easily show what they can do, find work near them or know who they are working for.

If you have a task

Finding someone for a repair, cleaning or delivery job means asking around, unclear prices and not knowing who will turn up.

How Oddify solves it

  • One trusted place. Post a task or offer your skills in Sinhala, Tamil or English.
  • The right match, close by. Jobs and helpers are matched by location and skill.
  • Confidence on both sides. Verified employers (proposed capability) and GPS tracking prototype are designed to show who is involved and where they are.
